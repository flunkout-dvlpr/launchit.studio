# Policy

Hard eligibility rules for what's worth surfacing in a price-cut writeup.
This is a filter, not a ranking, a listing either clears these or it
doesn't get a table row. Edit freely, no code required — same idea as
`keywords.md` for the email-reply flow.

Deliberately minimal: price is the only hard gate. No location or lot-size
requirement, any Houston-area price-cut listing under the ceiling is worth
a look, not just ones matching a specific saved search.

## Price

- Ceiling: **$900,000**. Anything above this doesn't clear policy
  regardless of how good the cut looks.

## What disqualifies a listing outright

- Price above the ceiling.
- The parsed data is incomplete (no address or no price) — don't guess at
  missing fields, exclude and note why.

## What this policy does NOT decide

- How good the cut itself is (that's `rubric.md` and the writeup, not a
  pass/fail gate).
- Whether to actually go see the place. This narrows the list down to
  ones worth a human looking at, nothing more.
